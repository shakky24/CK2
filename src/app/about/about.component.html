About Us

Cinema Kompany, established in 2011 by Harikrishnan R and Creative Director Avinash G Nair, has emerged as a trailblazer in the art of movie promotions. Formerly known as BOOM Cinemaz, we have played an instrumental role in successfully promoting over 18 films across Malayalam and Tamil cinema, earning a reputation for excellence and creativity.

In 2024, we embarked on an exciting new chapter by merging with DIB Global Entertainment, leading to our rebranding as Cinema Kompany. This strategic collaboration enables us to innovate at the forefront of the entertainment industry while continuing our legacy of impactful film promotions.

Looking ahead, we are expanding into the OTT space with the launch of our YouTube entertainment channel in mid-2025. Curated by the acclaimed writer Arun Sukumaran Nair, this platform will feature an eclectic mix of short films, unplugged performances, and exclusive content, delivering compelling stories to audiences worldwide.
Beyond digital innovation, Cinema Kompany remains a key player in movie charting for theaters in Kollam, with plans for further growth across Kerala. Our regional expertise, combined with a global outlook, positions us as a trusted partner for filmmakers and a source of inspiration for audiences.

At Cinema Kompany, we are passionate about storytelling and dedicated to crafting experiences that captivate and connect. With a steadfast commitment to professionalism, creativity, and innovation, we are reshaping the entertainment landscape, one project at a time.
